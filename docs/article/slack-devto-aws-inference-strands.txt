One Strands agent driving six ways to serve a model on AWS: Bedrock, a SageMaker endpoint, vLLM on EC2 g6 and g5g, and a hand-ported Gemma 4 on Inferentia2 and Trainium.
Same prompts on every backend, graded and timed in code, two full runs on the same day.
The same Inferentia2 image runs on Trainium unchanged. On the second run inf2 had no capacity in us-east-2 or us-east-1 and ran in us-west-2.
